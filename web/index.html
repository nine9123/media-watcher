Convert to avoid transcoding

Your browser cannot decode this video (), so every play re-encodes it on the server's CPU. Converting it once to 8-bit H.264 makes it play with a near-free remux instead. Audio tracks are kept; MKV files keep their subtitles and are replaced in place, so watch progress survives. Other containers get a new .mkv next to the original – delete the original afterwards.

Run in (the path inside the container; use the same folder on the NAS) with a full ffmpeg, while nothing is playing the file.
